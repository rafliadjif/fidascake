# FIDA'S Oven Fresh

Build a charming, fresh-from-oven ordering website for a fictional Indonesian home bakery called "FIDA'S" (Cake & Cookies). Demo

only. M1 engine + PRE-ORDER flavor: roti harian pesen sebelum jam tertentu, kue custom H-1; signature = JADWAL KELUAR OVEN.

BRAND- Name: FIDA'S — "Cake & Cookies"- Tagline (hero): "Masih anget pas sampe rumah lu."- Positioning: bakery rumahan batch kecil, roti keluar oven 2x sehari (jadwal dipajang), kue ulang tahun custom PO H-1, foto real tanpa filter food

styling berlebihan.- Audience: 22–40 keluarga muda & pekerja area [kota]; bosen roti pabrikan, takut kue custom zonk.- Mood: hangat, homey, menggoda — aroma oven kerasa dari layar.

ALL ON-SCREEN TEXT IN BAHASA INDONESIA.

DESIGN SYSTEM (oven-warm)- Colors: background butter cream #FCF6EA; primary cinnamon #9A5B2B; accent strawberry #D8555C; ink #33241A; section vanilla #F6EDDC.- Typography: soft charming display (Fraunces italic accents) + Nunito Sans body.- Style: foto close-up tekstur roti sobek & lelehan cokelat, steam illustration halus, rounded-2xl. Mobile-first.

PAGES: / , /menu , /kue-custom , /lokasi , /tentang , /keranjang , /checkout

GLOBAL COMPONENTS: navbar (Menu, Kue Custom, Lokasi) + cart badge + status pill 08.00–20.00 · order-type toggle Ambil Sendiri/Antar

(radius 5 km) · footer "© 2026 REMAH."
